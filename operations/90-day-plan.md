# First 90 days

Planning start: 2026-09-24; first release week starts September 28. Audience: 0–7 families; parent-child dancing is the constant. This is the first phase of the [1,000-subscriber / 4,000-hour growth plan](ypp-growth-plan.md), which controls the calendar and targets. The owner confirmed one polished original per week, five hours weekly and $50–80 monthly. Prove the production approach fits those limits; flag any cadence risk rather than reduce acceptance standards. The 26-week horizon and numerical scenarios are planning targets, not growth forecasts from the tiny baseline.

| Period | Work | Evidence required to advance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup, Sep 24–27 / first-week allowance | Confirm language/markets and inventory tool costs/credits against the agreed limits. Capture live Earn baseline. Finish rights/disclosure records. Prepare first two briefs and approve a finished sample. | Accepted sample, usable measurement setup, affordable tool path; no automatic release |
| Weeks 1–4 | Produce up to four distinct baseline songs. Keep cast, thumbnail convention and quality stable. Log song differences and review the model on October 25. | Production hours/cost, full playback QA, mature per-video windows; topic comparisons remain exploratory |
| Weeks 5–10 | Choose ONE experiment based on the baseline. Preregister three matched pairs across six original uploads and counterbalance order. | Same windows and traffic scope, adequate observations, intact guardrails; otherwise inconclusive |
| Weeks 11–13 | Three further originals; review matured pairs and reforecast inside weekly review time. | Target 13 new originals total; pending windows carried forward, no causal claim |
| Optional after week 8 | One session pilot only if eight accepted songs, a two-song release buffer and spare time/credits exist. | Does not displace the weekly original or exceed five hours/$80 ceilings; otherwise deferred |

The final releases may not have full 28-day fallback data by day 90. Carry those experiments forward as pending, rather than report them as completed. Shorts are deferred in the core plan; their watch hours do not count toward this route.

## Candidate briefs, not a committed upload queue

| Concept | Shared activity | Lead band | Learning purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clap & Copy | Parent claps a short pattern; child copies; roles reverse | 3–5 | Tests clarity and appeal of family mirroring |
| Freeze Together | Both dance, then hold a simple pose on a cue | 3–5 | Tests a comprehensible game loop |
| Twinkle Dance Party | Parents and children show gentle star/sky gestures to the approved song | 3–5 | Uses existing audio only after rights/audio review; not proof the song is finished |
| Stomp, Clap, Wave | Visible single-action verses followed by a shared chorus | 3–5 | Tests cue-demonstration alignment |
| Copy My Silly Move | Child leads; adult follows an easy choice of moves | 6–7 | Tests role reversal for the older activity band |
| Family Hello Dance | A greeting song with a simple seated or standing participation option | Adult selected, adaptable | Tests a recurring family occasion without developmental claims |

Future ideas can include counting, farm themes and seasonal celebrations, but a number/learning claim must be accurately delivered. Do not compare all pillars at once and declare the most-viewed one the causal winner.

## Weekly rhythm

Choose the brief and log the hypothesis; lock music; approve a short finished sample; produce and QA; release only within owner authorization; record costs and exact release state. At the planned review date, inspect matured data, update the results/cycle records and sync GitHub. The research records use existing local tools; video-production tools still need a demonstrated quality/cost fit inside the agreed budget.

The first 90-day operational criteria are a reusable accepted production style, a coherent small library within budget, trustworthy measurement, and at least one interpretable comparison or an honest inconclusive finding. The full growth plan includes an explicitly hypothetical goal path; replace its rate assumptions with observed evidence and reforecast after the first month.
