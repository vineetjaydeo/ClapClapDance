# Production and release standard — v1

Every new brief must say how kids and parents dance together, which activity level leads, and what one family activity the song delivers. The owner confirmed a 0–7 channel; individual songs need not demand the same participation from every age.

## Production gates

| Gate | Concrete evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Song lock | Approved full recording; lyric/transcript checked by listening; readable actions; stable rhythm; music provenance logged |
| Cast/style | Consistent parent and child references, expressions, hands, proportions, clothing and scene scale; no borrowed recognizable franchise characters |
| Finished sample | 10–15 seconds at intended final quality, including a hard action, readable lyric cue and actual song audio; owner acceptance recorded |
| Choreography | Cue, demonstration and time to follow; visible limbs; direction convention consistent; adults and children doing meaningful related actions |
| Full render | No identity drift, malformed bodies, unexplained cuts, broken movement, wrong lyrics or misleading scene logic; complete playback checked |
| Audio | Final output auditioned against the approved song; no altered tune, missing verse, tempo drift, clipping or drowned-out vocals |
| Delivery | Native 1920×1080 or better for new 16:9 masters where feasible; stable frame rate; captions and thumbnail legible small; actual playback checked on phone and TV-sized display |
| Release | Audience setting, current AI-use disclosure, rights, accurate title/description, playlist assignment and final file identity checked |

A file named “final,” a successful encoder, a motion test or a source-image approval is not acceptance of the completed video. Track technical pass and creative acceptance separately.

## Editorial defaults to validate

- Music and shared parent-child activity begin quickly; no long logo-only opening.
- One clear action at a time before combining actions. Leave space for children to respond.
- Hold a full or useful body view during demonstration; use close-ups for expression without hiding the movement being taught.
- Repeat the core action with a meaningful variation, rather than requiring constant new movements.
- Clear sound and calm visual organization around energetic music. Avoid flashes, startling volume changes or manipulative distress.
- Provide smaller or seated participation options where the activity allows. Avoid lifts, stunts, fast repeated spins and pressure to continue.
- End the activity intentionally. Invite a grown-up to choose what comes next only when useful; do not direct children toward purchases or personal-data forms.

These are creative operating defaults. They are not medical/developmental claims or a validated “optimal” song length, BPM or edit interval.

## Rights record per song

Record separately: composition/melody source; lyric source/adaptation; recording/master; performer/voice permissions; AI tool and plan/date; commercial-use terms evidence; input-image permissions; character/likeness rights; font/stock license; and any restrictions on redistribution or Content ID. Keep contract copies and private identities outside public Git. Use `unverified` until evidence exists.

Traditional material and newly generated recordings require separate review. Do not make blanket exclusive-copyright claims without support. See [copyright](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797466?hl=en) and [AI-use disclosure](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en). AI music is specifically listed in the disclosure examples; cartoon visuals do not settle the audio question.

## Release record

Save the approved brief, final asset hash, title, thumbnail version/hash, description, publication timestamp/timezone, video ID, audience setting, AI disclosure review, rights status, production hours/cost, and experiment ID. Verify the public link after an authorized release. Log any later packaging change with its timestamp so analytics can be interpreted.

The current task authorizes research and repository synchronization. It has not authorized a new upload, live metadata change, ad campaign or external message.
