# Benchmark observations and sampling protocol

Reviewed 2026-09-24. This initial pass reads creator-owned pages and descriptions. It is not a frame-by-frame video study, a representative market sample, or a performance ranking. No competitor analytics were accessed.

| Primary source | Observed pattern | ClapClapDance hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Super Simple: Dance and Stop](https://supersimple.com/song/dance-and-stop/) | A repeated movement-and-stop structure, with a small set of named actions | Predictable cue/repeat structure may make parent-child participation easier |
| [GoNoodle: Enmoción Freeze](https://www.gonoodle.com/videos/lYOmA2/freeze) | Guided dance, freeze prompts and repeated sequences in its transcript; classroom/grade categories | Package a complete activity adults can select for a specific occasion |
| [The Learning Station: Pause Pause](https://www.learningstationmusic.com/blog/2020/12/02/pause-pause-%E2%99%AB-brain-breaks-%E2%99%AB-kids-move-and-freeze-song-by-the-learning-station-%E2%99%AB-with-santa-claus/) | Seasonal theme applied to a move/freeze activity with accompanying music resources | Seasonal variants can be useful after an evergreen activity format is understood |
| [Scratch Garden catalog](https://scratchgarden.com/videos/) | Distinct movement/activity entries and a catalog organized by topic, series and character | A navigable library can make the channel useful beyond a single song |
| [Danny Go official site](https://www.dannygo.net/) | Video discovery plus a music, live-event and merchandise presence | Broader IP opportunities exist as a later-stage model; this does not establish their economics |

These patterns support research directions, not claims that copying them will cause success. Do not copy melodies, lyrics, characters, choreographic expression, thumbnails or branding. The family-pair concept still requires its own validation.

## Next benchmark pass, preregistered method

Before expanding the sample, freeze the date, channels and selection rule. Proposed method: the five most recent eligible original long-form dance/activity songs from each of the five creators above at the cutoff. Exclude Shorts, livestreams, advertisements and compilations from this cohort; record exclusions. If a channel has fewer eligible videos, record the actual count instead of substituting its greatest hits. Add a separate purposive sample of smaller/newer channels with disclosed selection criteria before making claims about new-channel feasibility.

Record URL, publication time, capture time, runtime, language, original/compilation status, public views at capture, thumbnail subject, adults/children present, first music/action time, move visibility, cue structure and ending. Code video-based fields only after watching the relevant parts. Use `unknown` when unavailable.

Normalize public views only descriptively by video age, and do not treat views/day as launch velocity or as a fair causal comparison across channels. Older videos, seasonal releases, established audiences, paid distribution and localization differ. Public metrics cannot provide competitor CTR, retention, traffic source mix, RPM or repeat family use.

Have a second review pass check ambiguous coding if feasible. Publish the selection/exclusion log alongside results. No full sample has been collected yet.
