# Optional adult feedback protocol

Recruitment and sending are not authorized by this template. If the owner chooses a pilot, ask only consenting adults for voluntary observations. Do not contact children, collect their names, require recordings, or offer rewards for YouTube views/likes/subscriptions.

A small convenience panel (for example 5–8 adults) is a usability check, not representative market evidence. Count adults/sessions explicitly and record recruitment bias. Broad activity bands are optional; no birth dates or identities.

Ask the adult:

1. What did you expect the activity to be from the title and cover?
2. Was it obvious what you and the child could do together? At what point was it unclear?
3. Could you follow the movement and hear the cue comfortably?
4. Did anyone choose to participate, stop, or ask for the song again? “Not observed” is a valid answer.
5. Would you choose this again for a real occasion? Which occasion, and what would prevent you?

Do not ask them to keep watching to improve retention. Allow stopping. Store only anonymous aggregate notes outside public Git until reviewed. A stated intention to reuse is not observed repeat use; neither establishes a developmental benefit.
